01 — Profile

Two disciplines,
many languages,
one stance.

Law is the language in which a society negotiates what may be expected of its members. Economics is the language in which it allocates its resources. To work fluently in both is to understand contracts, transactions and accountability not as separate worlds, but as two sides of the same coin.

I read law at the University of Bern (BLaw 2024, MLaw 2025) and now serve as a Trainee Lawyer at Burkhalter Rechtsanwälte in Bern and Zurich. In parallel, I am pursuing an MSc in Business Administration with a specialisation in Corporate Finance, and a CAS in International Law & Economics at the World Trade Institute.

My background as an accredited court interpreter for the Canton of Bern shaped how I read texts: precisely, with attention to nuance, and aware that the choice of a single word can decide a case. The same care underlies how I draft, advise and present.

07 — Code

What I stand by.

i.

Care

An argument that holds. A sentence that lands. A detail that was not overlooked — precisely where no one would think to check. Because what is made invisible is what makes the difference in the end.

— diligentia
ii.

Discretion

What is shared in confidence stays in confidence. As a default, not on request. Professional ethics as a stance — not a phrase.

— discretio
iii.

Clarity

Direct in tone, precise in substance. If a matter cannot be explained simply, it has either not been understood — or is meant to be obscured. Both are to be avoided.

— claritas
iv.

Reliability

What is promised is kept. Deadlines, commitments, confidentiality. Whoever relies on it should never be disappointed.

— fides
